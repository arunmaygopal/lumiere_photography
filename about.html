Behind the camera

A photographer, a curious observer and someone who loves collecting stories through images.

HELLO, I'M THE PHOTOGRAPHER, JENNIFER CHARDET

I look for the little things that make a story special.

My interest in photography started with the simple habit of taking pictures while travelling and spending time with friends. Over time, I became interested in light, composition and the way a photograph can bring back a feeling.

Today, through Lumière, I work with individuals, couples and small businesses to create photographs that feel natural and personal. I prefer relaxed sessions, genuine expressions and images that do not need too much staging.
